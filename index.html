About Us

Proprietor: Koushik Ghosh

Maya Enterprise delivers reliable, high-precision repair and overhauling / servicing solutions for industries across India. From steel and power to cement, telecom, packaging, pharma and oil & gas, if your plant runs on electrical and electronic controls, we are built to keep it running.

We carry out on-site servicing of AC drives by deputing an experienced team: drives are dismantled, parts cleaned, cooling fans serviced, conformal coating applied to PCBs, heatsink paste applied to IGBT bases, then reassembled and tested.
